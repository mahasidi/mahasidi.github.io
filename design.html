Self Reflection Photo Journal

I created a personal photo journal to document my journey of self-exploration during my first year of college and I used this as an outlet to remind myself of what's most meaningful to me. Amist the chaos and confusion of college life, this journal gave me an opportunity hit pause on life and truly reflect on my surroundings and how far I've come. All illustrations were created using Adobe Illustrator, the page layout was organized using Adobe InDesign, and all photos were taken by me.

Spoiler Alert

Spoiler Alert is an app designed to help college students and young adults reduce food waste by racking expiration dates and suggesting diverse, time-saving recipes. Informed by Wizard of Oz testing, focus groups, and interviews, the app encourages mindful consumption and empowers users to make the most of their groceries while balancing a busy lifestyle.

Extra-Continental Biographies

This project is a collection of stories about refugees and migrants striving for a better life. Through four personal narratives from Aden Yemen, New Delhi India, Yaoundé Cameroon, and Tehran Iran, the book highlights the challenges, resilience, and sacrifices of those forced to leave their homes. Focusing on typographic elements and using structure to aid storytelling, Extra-Continental Biographies provides insight into the realities of migration and the pursuit of new beginnings.

Radiance is a thoughtfully designed skincare companion that helps you navigate the world of skincare with expert insights and interactive tools. Mining an Archive: Time Magazine

This project analyzes the demographics of Time's Person of the Year, highlighting its lack of diversity. Using information design, it contrasts the Man of the Year era with the fact that only 12% of honorees have been women. This juxtaposition not only emphasizes historical biases but also reflects broader societal values and attitudes over time.
