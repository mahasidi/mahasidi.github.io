04

PeaceJam Conference

My team and I presented at the PeaceJam Southeastern conference in Tallahassee, Florida. Our goal for this presentation was to raise awareness about the refugee crisis globally. My first-hand research consisted of visiting the Stewart Detention Center in Lumpkin, Georgia. With the help of El Refugio (A Georgia-based organization that provides support to individuals who've family members have been detained), I was able to converse with men who were being detained and awaiting their trial. In the presentation, I was able to amplify their voices and call for action.
